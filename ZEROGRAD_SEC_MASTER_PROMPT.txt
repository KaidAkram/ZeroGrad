Five required studies:
E1 Sample efficiency and end-to-end compute: N∈{0,50,200,1000,5000,20000} where meaningful; macro F1, balanced accuracy, class F1, multiclass one-vs-rest AUROC when defined, calibration ECE, compile time, optimizer time, inference p50/p95 latency, tokens/sec or samples/sec, peak memory.
